About Me

A creator at the starting line.

My name is Night11r. I'm a young entrepreneur and aspiring game developer who has spent the last few years learning, experimenting and building on my own — driven by curiosity rather than a classroom.

I've worked on multiple personal projects independently, learned the technical side of development by doing, and kept pushing into new territory: gameplay logic, systems, game design, and the entrepreneurial side of turning an idea into something real.

This site is not a portfolio of finished commercial games. It's the beginning of a larger journey. I haven't released my first major official title yet — deliberately. I'm looking for the right team of passionate people to help turn my ideas into something much bigger than anything I could build alone.

What I bring is vision, an appetite for hard problems, and the willingness to learn whatever the project demands. What I'm looking for is people who want to build something real with me.
